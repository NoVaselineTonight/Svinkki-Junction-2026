## What was confirmed
- Latest survey year: 2022 (https://stat.fi/en/statistics/ktutk).
- Current database: StatFin__ktutk, Households' consumption (https://pxdata.stat.fi/PXWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin/StatFin__ktutk/?tablelist=true). Archived: StatFin_Passiivi__ktutk (https://pxdata.stat.fi/PXWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin_Passiivi/StatFin_Passiivi__ktutk/?tablelist=true).
- The 2022 page lists charts (no values in the fetched text): consumption shares by income bracket (2022); monthly expenditure by household type (2012 to 2022); monthly expenditure by consumption item and household size and structure (2022); monthly expenditure by degree of urbanisation and consumption item (2022). These are the likely source for household-type breakdowns, but the numbers still need to be pulled from the PxWeb tables.
- Survey results page: https://stat.fi/kul/results (redirects to a Power BI view; not read).
- Survey page: https://stat.fi/en/surveys/kul.

Annual figures are monthly values times 12, so state which was used.

## Caveats
- Household averages are not one person's bank account. They are averages across all households in a survey year and include non-cash items and imputed values.
- Survey data covers 2022 only at the time of this lookup, not 2024 or 2026.
- Survey figures are about 85 percent of the national-accounts consumption total (per the search snippet, not verified on a primary page).
- Categories may not map one-to-one onto the requested COICOP-style groups.
