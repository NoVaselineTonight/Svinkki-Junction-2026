## On-camera treatment

- S-Bank green and lime palette, serif-free typography, warm off-white, quiet grid lines.
- Alternating phone lean and side-view, with inset crop controlled by each scene.
- Screenshots stay upright on the phone; only the whole device is rotated to preserve UI proportions.
- Energetic cuts, eased zooms and subtle vertical panning, with the widget given the most time.
- Product-authentic screens only: no fabricated typing, alerts or money movements.

**Narration:** local Windows synthetic voice. **Music:** original synthesized electronic backing. **Output:** `output/Svinkki-demo-1080p.mp4`; poster, editable captions and production source included.
